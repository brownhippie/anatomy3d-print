The server teaches the builder on its own: it builds your study photos, scores every model automatically, moves one setting at a time and keeps what scores better. With "Teaching itself" on it judges each finished study, applies it only if it passes and starts the next; otherwise nothing is used until you press Apply. "Back to the code's own values" undoes any of it.
